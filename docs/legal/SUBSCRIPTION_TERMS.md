## 2. Introductory AI generations (Free and Standard)

- Each **eligible account** receives **three** introductory AI recipe generations **once**. An account is eligible when it has a verified email address and has not received them before.
- A generation is counted only when it **succeeds**, meaning you receive recipe results. If a generation fails because of a technical problem, or the provider is unavailable or times out, it is **not** counted.
- Introductory generations:
  - are **not renewed**, do not expire while your account exists, have no cash value and cannot be transferred;
  - remain available if you move to Standard;
  - are not used up while you are on Premium, because Premium uses its own quota;
  - are removed when you delete your account.
- They are intended to be used once per person. Creating extra accounts to get more is not allowed (see the **Acceptable Use Policy**). **[OWNER TO CONFIRM: any anti-abuse measure, consistent with the decision not to keep email addresses after account deletion.]**

## 3. Premium fair-use quotas

AI features cost us money each time they are used, so Premium includes AI use **within fair-use quotas**. It is **not unlimited**. The quotas are:

- **[OWNER TO CONFIRM: up to 100 AI generations per calendar month — provisional]**;
- **[OWNER TO CONFIRM: up to 10 AI generations per hour — provisional]**.

The app shows how much of your monthly quota you have used. If you reach a quota, AI features pause until the time shown, and everything else keeps working. Failed generations do not count. We may also pause AI features briefly for everyone if our provider has an outage or our spending safeguards are reached. We will show a message when this happens.

We will tell you in advance before reducing quotas for an existing subscription, and you may cancel if you do not agree.

## 4. Buying a subscription through Apple

- Subscriptions are sold through Apple's App Store using **In-App Purchase**. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase.
- Before you subscribe, the app shows the plan name, what it includes, the **price and billing period** from Apple, and links to these terms, the Terms & Conditions and the Privacy Policy.
- Subscriptions are **monthly** and **renew automatically** at the same price each month **unless you turn off auto-renewal at least 24 hours before the end of the current period**. Apple charges the renewal within the 24 hours before the period ends.
- Apple handles payment. We never see your card details.
- **Price changes:** if the price of your subscription changes, Apple will notify you. Where Apple's rules require it, Apple will ask you to agree to the new price; if you do not agree, your subscription will not renew.

## 5. Managing, upgrading and downgrading

- **Manage Subscription:** in the app (**Settings → Subscription → Manage subscription**), or on iPhone in **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions**. [PENDING IMPLEMENTATION: LS-02.]
- **Upgrade (Standard → Premium):** takes effect immediately. Apple adjusts what you pay for the rest of the current period.
- **Downgrade (Premium → Standard):** takes effect at your next renewal date. You keep Premium until then.
- **Restore Purchases:** if you reinstall the app or use a new iPhone, tap **Restore Purchases** to reconnect your Apple subscription to your IntelliShop account. [PENDING IMPLEMENTATION: LS-01.]
  - Restoring does **not** grant extra introductory generations.
  - A subscription bought with one Apple Account can be moved to a different IntelliShop account only after we have securely checked that the purchase belongs to you. The previous account then returns to Free.

## 6. Cancellation, expiry and billing problems

- **Cancelling** turns off auto-renewal through Apple (section 5). You keep your plan until the end of the period you have paid for.
- **When a paid plan ends,** your account returns to **Free**:
  - **we do not delete anything**, and all your lists, pantry items and saved recipes stay available to view, edit, tick off and delete;
  - Free limits apply only to **new** lists and items. For example, if you have 5 lists, you can keep and use them all, but you cannot create a new list until you are below the Free limit;
  - any unused introductory generations remain;
  - Premium-only features stop.
- **Billing problems:** if Apple cannot take a renewal payment, you may keep your plan for a short grace period while Apple retries. If payment is not recovered, your account returns to Free. It is restored automatically if Apple later collects the payment.
- **Deleting your account does not cancel your Apple subscription.** Cancel it first in your Apple settings. The account deletion screen reminds you of this and links to Apple's subscription settings. See the **Account & Data Deletion Policy**.

## 7. Refunds

- Because Apple takes payment, **refund requests for App Store purchases are made to Apple**, following Apple's instructions at support.apple.com/118223 or through **Settings → Subscription → Request a refund** in the app ([PENDING IMPLEMENTATION: LS-02]). Apple decides refund requests under its own policies.
- If Apple refunds a subscription, paid features end when the refund is processed. Your data is not deleted.
- **Your legal rights are not affected.** If the Service is faulty or not as described, you may have rights to a repair, replacement, price reduction or refund under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 (see **Terms & Conditions**, section 10). Please contact us as well as Apple so we can help.

## 8. Cooling-off period

**[LEGAL REVIEW: required before publication.]**

Under the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013, consumers usually have **14 days** to cancel a contract for digital content or services bought online. For digital content that starts immediately, the right to cancel is lost once supply begins, **only if** you:
- gave your **express consent** to start straight away, and
- **acknowledged** that you would lose the right to cancel,

and we confirmed this to you in writing.

## 9. New subscription rules (expected 1 January 2027)

The UK Government has laid draft regulations before Parliament: the Consumer Subscription Contracts (Cancellation and Information) Regulations 2026, made under the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024. **They are not yet approved.** If approved, they are expected to apply to subscriptions taken out **on or after 1 January 2027**. They would introduce, among other things:
- reminder notices before certain renewal payments;
- cooling-off periods after renewals;
- easy ways to end a subscription.
