# Pricing & Supermarket Data

> **Draft for owner review — not published.** This page reflects the current position: **IntelliShop has no licensed source of supermarket prices.** It must be updated before any supermarket price feature is launched.

## 1. Where prices in IntelliShop come from

IntelliShop does **not** currently show live, official or automatically collected supermarket prices.

Any price you see in the app is one of these:

| Type | Label in the app | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Your price** | "Your price" | A price **you** typed in, for example from a receipt or shelf label |
| **Estimate** | "Estimated" | A total calculated from the prices you typed in. Items without a price are not included, and the app shows how many items are missing a price |

[PENDING IMPLEMENTATION: price labels and the "items without a price" count, milestone M4 (List Detail).]

IntelliShop does **not** use AI to guess, estimate or invent prices.

## 2. Why your prices may differ from what you pay

Prices you entered earlier may no longer be correct. What you pay can differ because of:

- price changes since you entered the price;
- **online and in-store prices** being different, and differing between stores of the same chain;
- **promotions, multi-buy offers and loyalty-card prices**, which may apply only to some customers;
- **delivery, service, bag or minimum-basket charges** for online orders;
- **product availability**: an item may be out of stock or replaced with a substitute;
- **weight-based items**, whose final price depends on the actual weight;
- **regional differences**.

Always check the price at the till or in the retailer's checkout before you pay.

## 3. Supermarket comparison

The Premium plan is designed to include store and basket comparisons **only where a licensed, reliable price source is available**.

**At the moment no such source is available, so comparisons are not offered.** **[OWNER TO CONFIRM before launch.]**

Before any comparison is offered, this page will name:
- the source of the price data;
- the retailers and areas it covers;
- how often it is updated.

Each comparison will show:
- when each price was last updated;
- how many items in your list could be matched;
- which items are missing from the comparison.

We will not show a saving or a "cheapest store" unless the data supports it. We do not guarantee savings.

## 4. No partnerships or endorsements

IntelliShop is independent. It has **no partnership, sponsorship or affiliation** with any supermarket or retailer, and no retailer has approved or endorsed it. Retailer names, if they appear in your own lists or notes, belong to their owners. **[OWNER TO CONFIRM: update if any partnership is agreed.]**

We do not receive payment for showing or recommending any retailer or product.

## 5. Areas covered

IntelliShop is available only in the **United Kingdom**. Any future price data will state which parts of the UK it covers.
