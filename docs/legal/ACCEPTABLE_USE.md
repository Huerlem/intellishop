# Acceptable Use Policy

> **Draft for owner review — not published.**

This policy forms part of our **Terms & Conditions**. It explains what you must not do when using IntelliShop. We want the Service to stay safe, fair and affordable for everyone.

## 1. Use IntelliShop lawfully and for yourself

Do not:
- use IntelliShop for anything unlawful, fraudulent or harmful;
- add content you have no right to use, or content that is unlawful, such as another person's photo used without permission;
- use the Service for a business, or resell or rent access to it.

## 2. Do not get around limits, credits or payment

Do not:
- create multiple accounts, or use someone else's account, to get extra introductory AI generations, avoid plan limits or avoid paying;
- tamper with the app, its data or network traffic to unlock paid features, change your usage counts or bypass fair-use quotas;
- share paid access in ways Apple's Family Sharing or our terms do not allow;
- misuse Restore Purchases or Apple's refund process, for example by claiming purchases that are not yours.

## 3. Do not misuse the AI features

Do not:
- try to make the AI produce content unrelated to shopping, food or cooking;
- try to extract our instructions to the AI, or make the AI ignore its safety checks;
- try to make the AI produce harmful, dangerous, hateful, sexual or illegal content;
- try to use IntelliShop as a general-purpose AI service;
- send large volumes of requests that serve no genuine household purpose.

You must also follow the AI provider's usage rules that apply through us. **[OWNER TO CONFIRM: link to the provider's acceptable use policy once contracted.]**

## 4. Do not attack or overload the Service

Do not:
- try to access other people's accounts or data, or areas of the Service you are not authorised to use;
- probe, scan or test the security of the Service without our written permission (section 6);
- interfere with or disrupt the Service, for example with denial-of-service attacks, malware or excessive automated traffic;
- use bots, scrapers or other automated tools to access the Service or collect data from it;
- copy, decompile or reverse-engineer the app, except where the law allows you to.

## 5. Respect third-party services

IntelliShop relies on services provided by Apple, Google and others. Do not use IntelliShop in a way that breaks their terms, such as Apple's App Store terms or rules on In-App Purchases.

## 6. Reporting security problems

If you find a security weakness, please email **[OWNER TO CONFIRM: security/support email]** with details. Do not access other people's data, and do not publish the problem until we have had a reasonable chance to fix it. We will not take action against anyone who reports a problem in good faith and follows this section.

## 7. What happens if this policy is broken

What we do depends on how serious the problem is. We may:
- warn you;
- remove content;
- reverse credits or quota obtained in breach of this policy;
- temporarily limit features;
- suspend or close your account (see **Terms & Conditions**, section 12).

Where reasonable, we will tell you why and give you a chance to respond. We may report unlawful activity to the authorities.
