## 3. Privacy requests

You can ask us to access, correct, delete, restrict or transfer your personal information, or object to how we use it, by emailing **[OWNER TO CONFIRM: privacy/support email]**. Many of these you can also do yourself in the app (see above).

- We may ask you to confirm your identity.
- We will respond within **one month**. If your request is complex, we may extend this by up to two more months and will tell you why.

## 4. Complaints

### 4.1 How to complain

Email **[OWNER TO CONFIRM: support email]** with "Complaint" in the subject, or use **Help → Privacy request or complaint** in the app ([PENDING IMPLEMENTATION]). Please tell us:
- what happened;
- when it happened;
- what you would like us to do.

### 4.2 What we will do

1. **Acknowledge** your complaint within **[OWNER TO CONFIRM: e.g. 5 working days]**. For complaints about your personal information, we will acknowledge them within **30 days** at the latest, as the law requires.
2. **Look into it** without undue delay, ask you for any information we need, and keep you updated.
3. **Reply in writing** with our findings and what we will do, normally within **[OWNER TO CONFIRM: e.g. 4 weeks]**. If it will take longer, we will tell you why and when to expect an answer.

### 4.3 If you are not satisfied

- **Personal information complaints:** you can complain to the **Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)** at ico.org.uk, telephone 0303 123 1113. The ICO usually expects you to raise the matter with us first.
- **Other consumer complaints:** you can get free advice from Citizens Advice (citizensadvice.org.uk) or, in Scotland, Consumer Advice Scotland. You may also be able to take your claim to court.
- **App Store purchases:** problems with payment or billing can also be raised with Apple.
